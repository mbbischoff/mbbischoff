[mb bischoff](https://mbbischoff.com) ♥︎ makes [poems](https://chromato.graphics/), [pictures](https://mbbischoff.com/pictures/), & [programs](https://mbbischoff.com/projects) in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn).

it writes and publishes poetry at [chromatographics](https://chromato.graphics/), where each poem has a [color](https://chromatographics.com/colors/). its writing has also appeared in [beestung](https://beestungmag.com/issue12/in-your-ideal-world-what-does-the-future-of-gender-look-like-and-t4t-by-matthew-bischoff/), [Poetry is Gay](https://the-town-cryer.myshopify.com/products/poetry-is-gay-the-zine-issue-2), [Sex Change and the City](https://girldadpress.com/scatc), [Dykes and Dolls](https://dykesanddolls.substack.com/p/dykes-and-dolls-issue-20), [New Session](https://web.issue4.anewsession.com/story/zig_zag_cafe_lease_this_space/1), and [Alchemize](https://tinycutlery.com/shop/p/alchemize-a-trans-poetry-anthology).

she makes [photographs](https://mbbischoff.com/pictures/) on film, usually of the people and objects she loves. she wants you to see what she sees. she wants you to fall in love.

mb co-founded [Lickability](https://lickability.com/), a software studio that makes apps for clients like [The Atlantic](https://theatlantic.com/), [RevenueCat](https://revenuecat.com/), and [The New Yorker](https://newyorker.com/). before making that her full-time job in 2015, she worked at [The New York Times](https://nytimes.com/)[^1] and [tumblr](https://tumblr.com/)[^2] as a software engineer, engineering manager, and product manager. she studied [HCI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human%E2%80%93computer_interaction) at [NJIT](https://njit.edu/) before [dropping out](https://mbbischoff.com/don-t-go-to-college/) to make things.

along the way, she’s [given talks](https://mbbischoff.com/talks/) and [interviews](https://mbbischoff.com/projects/#press) about the craft.

it works on [Namesake](https://namesake.fyi/), which helps people change their name, and previously volunteered with [Trans Lifeline](https://translifeline.org/) as a hotline operator.

mb has been [posting](https://mbbischoff.com/posts/) and [podcasting](https://mbbischoff.com/projects/#podcasts) since 2003. she hosted [Absolutely Crushed](https://absolutelycrushed.com/), where she and [Syd Rose](https://instagram.com/syd_andyson/) gossiped about celebrity crushes.

its favorite color is [red](https://pantone.com/color-finder/20-0069-tpm).
